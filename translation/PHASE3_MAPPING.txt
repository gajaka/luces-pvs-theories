  source ~/.zprofile

  python3 translation/check_phase3.py --files ppg_mt_correspondence

reported formulas and TCCs, matching totals, and no proof errors in the log.

SIGMA-ALGEBRA ADAPTER AND COORDINATE BLOCKS

ppg_nasa_sigma proves the equivalence between sequential sigma closure and
NASALib's countable-family sigma_algebra? predicate. It constructs an
enumeration of any countable family and an injection for a sequence range,
including empty families, within the project. asNasa and asProject preserve
the event family exactly; their TCCs and both roundtrip identities are proved.
The adapter uses NASALib definitions and does not rely on its countable-image
or countable-union theorems. Equality with NASALib's generated_sigma_algebra
constructor is not included: its library TCC/dependency chain still needs
verification. No probability measure is constructed by this adapter.

ppg_product_restriction represents a block assignment as a dependent function
on the actual subtype (F). extendDefault updates that block and keeps the base
outside it. Its restriction is the original partial assignment. Updating a
disjoint block leaves the first restriction unchanged. A block-dependent
event is exactly the inverse image of blockEvent through restriction, and
blockEvent is independent of the base used for its definition. Both maps and
the induced block event are measurable for the generated product sigma
algebras, not just for discrete spaces.

These four mapped source theorems hold for every base assignment and every
block, so they specialize to the source finite block and its probability-
selected basePoint. No global inhabitance hypothesis or measure/independence
axiom is added. ppg_probability_coordinates now constructs basePoint from
coordinate probability laws; ppg_probability_extension specializes restriction,
factorization, and measurability to that base.

COORDINATE PROBABILITY LAWS

ppg_probability_measure defines a normalized NASALib finite measure on the
measurable events of S: finite_measure?(mu) and mu(fullset) = 1. The imported
predicate includes the full countable-additivity law, expressed as convergence
of partial sums for every disjoint measurable sequence. It is not replaced
by finite additivity or normalization alone. The carrier remains TYPE, not
TYPE+. Empty-set mass zero and full-set mass one prove that a carrier with
such a measure has a point. basePoint uses that proved fact to make a choice.

ppg_probability_series proves the partial sum and convergence of a sequence
with one nonzero term directly from NASALib's recursive sum and convergence
definitions. ppg_probability_dirac uses these results to construct a point
measure and prove its countable additivity. ppg_probability_map constructs
pushforward through any measurable map, proves inverse-image preservation
of disjointness and countable unions, and proves the resulting normalization
and countable additivity. No pushforward law is assumed.

ppg_probability_coordinates permits a separate sigma algebra and probability
law on each actual subtype (domain(i)). It constructs a dependent assignment
without a global nonemptiness assumption. ppg_probability_extension proves
restriction, outside-block values, event factorization, and measurability for
the resulting canonical extension. ppg_mt_coordinate_laws defines the law at
(n,v) as the law of v for every natural n, proves time invariance, and builds
a constant baseLog whose fresh and initial states equal baseState.

This interface assigns nonnegative real mass to measurable events. An outer
measure on all subsets has not been constructed. The adapter from coordinate
subtype sigma algebras to the ambient-Value productSigma representation also
remains to be proved before constructing the product of these laws. The
infinite product, its marginals, and independence remain open.

REPRESENTATION AND ASSUMPTIONS

C, V, and Value are ordinary PVS TYPE parameters, allowing empty carriers.
MTState is the dependent function type [v: V -> (domain(v))]. Coordinate
domains need not coincide; tagging values supports different Lean value types.
No global inhabitance premise is added. Individual quantified states or sample
tables provide inhabitants only in statements that already have those inputs.

LogSpace is [p: [nat,V] -> (domain(p`2))]. Its index is unbounded. Slot zero
supplies random initialization; resampling variable v reads its previous local
counter plus one. The counter increments only for the chosen footprint.
There is no fixed time horizon or global-step substitution for local counters.

The list scanned by pickFirstViolated and its fallback are explicit parameters.
Instantiating them with Lean's enumeration and default gives the source policy.
completeEnumeration requires coverage of C where the source uses Finset.univ;
the more general list lemmas also permit duplicates and incomplete lists.
The mt_wf premise is the original MTProcess dependency field: bad(c,s) depends
only on vars(c). It is not an additional probabilistic assumption.

A full finite universe parameter U with forall x: U(x) represents Fintype.
It is present only in the theories requiring finite variables, constraints,
or states. The generic trajectory, constant-table step, and reachability
definitions do not impose these finiteness conditions.

GTree has a finite set of natural-list addresses and a label function.
gt_valid? expresses root membership and prefix closure. Attachment chooses a
maximum-depth candidate and a fresh child address. tau_build scans the source
log backwards with natural truncated subtraction; tau_C uses t-1 steps.
The additional support port does not mark the older Growing module complete.
birthStep is the least stage at which an address occurs. Definitions are
totalized outside their source subtypes; mapped claims retain the membership
or nonempty-candidate guards under which the source uses those definitions.

MeasurableSpace uses the standard empty-set, complement, and countable-union
laws. generated is the intersection of the sigma algebras containing the
given generators. StateSigma and TableSigma are generated by coordinate
cylinders, rather than equipped with the full power-set sigma algebra.
The measurable product and countable-selection lemmas prove the coordinate
map results for these actual sigma algebras. ppg_measurable_pair likewise
constructs the binary product from its two projection families, for joint
resampling measurability. Full power-set sigma algebras are used for event
labels and natural counters, matching the source discrete measurable spaces.

No probability measure on the infinite table is constructed in this package.
In particular, measurability is not being counted as a proof of independence
or a probability bound. The installed-library review found binary products
and measure-extension support, but no ready construction matching this
arbitrary-index product of the source probability spaces. That construction
and its marginal laws remain proof work; they are not assumed as axioms.

COMPLETED MODULES

PPGraphMoserTardosRandomTrajectory (10/10)
  ppg_mt_policy_measurable proves measurability of the concrete first-violated
  scan and of resampling jointly in its two state arguments, including the
  recursive resampleChain. ppg_mt_trajectory_measurable proves measurability
  of the actual randStep state and each variable's local counter by induction.
  The measurableBad predicate is precisely the source hbad condition.

  Coordinate-wise resampling measurability allows the proof to avoid any
  additional enumeration-completeness premise: the selected label is a
  measurable function even with a partial scan and explicit fallback. The
  source finite complete enumeration is included. Reading a measurable local
  counter uses the already proved countable-selection lemma; no fixed horizon
  is imposed, and the source bounded-evaluation theorem remains available.

PPGraphMoserTardosRandomInitialization (3/3)
  ppg_mt_trajectory_measurable proves the same state/counter conjunction for
  every measurable table-dependent initialization, preserving hbad and hinit.
  stepMeasurable names exactly that conjunction; it does not replace the
  generated product sigma algebra. advance is the actual randStep successor
  expression, with its equality to randStep proved before induction. The
  slot-zero initialization is an explicit corollary. No independence premise
  is added, and no claim about future independence follows from measurability.

PPGraphMoserTardosCheckOrder (14/14)
  ppg_mt_check_order proves maximum-depth selection, fresh-address membership,
  preservation of old labels, monotone domains, and same-depth independence.
  ppg_mt_tree supplies the concrete recursive tree operations.

PPGraphMoserTardosCheck (1/1)
  ppg_mt_check defines localSet from actual deeper vertices using a variable,
  localCount as its cardinality, and checkState by the corresponding table
  reads. The bad-event result preserves the MT dependency condition.

PPGraphMoserTardosCheckBirth (22/22)
  ppg_mt_birth proves first appearance, stable labels, unique birth stages,
  and the relation between birth order and depth for a shared variable.
  ppg_mt_check and ppg_mt_check_birth prove strict local-count ordering and
  injectivity of the table coordinates used by distinct relevant vertices.

PPGraphMoserTardosCorrespondence (9/9)
  ppg_mt_selection proves faithful violated-event selection. The local-count
  proof constructs the bijection from deeper vertices to earlier uses of a
  variable, indexed by birthTime-1. Its cardinality is the recursive process
  counter. The result includes t=0. ppg_mt_correspondence then relates the
  actual state to table reads and proves tauCheck along a running trajectory.
  The original initialization-alignment and running-prefix premises remain.

PPGraphMoserTardosPastIndependence (2/2)
  ppg_mt_random_process proves equality of the step and state trajectories
  when two tables agree through time t, using the local counter bound.
  These are deterministic dependence results, as in the source module.

PPGraphMoserTardosConstantLog (9/9)
  Generic constant-table and resampling equalities live in
  ppg_mt_random_process. With a full finite variable universe,
  ppg_mt_constant_log proves strict descent of the actual mismatch set when
  the current state is bad and the target state is good. Natural rank descent
  yields a good state within the initial mismatch cardinality, hence within
  the number of variables. No probability or almost-sure claim is inferred.

PPGraphMoserTardosInfeasibility (9/9)
  ppg_mt_infeasibility connects actual trajectory reachability to existence of
  a good state, infeasibility, and minimal cores. ppg_mt_finite_infeasibility
  adds the finite-state goodCount equivalences and a repair witness. Full
  finite universes are explicit at the corresponding source assumptions.

EXAMPLES AND REMAINING WORK

ppg_phase3_examples uses two variables with different value domains. The
second step reads local slot one of the second variable, whose global slot
two deliberately differs. The proofs check both states and counters, reach
a good state, and verify the tree check on that actual trajectory. They also
prove that its bad-event sets are measurable and that the run is measurable
when initialized from slot zero or slot seven. The latter checks the full
measurable-initialization theorem, whose premise allows future-table reads;
it is not a future-independence or termination theorem.
ppg_phase3_empty checks an empty variable carrier and empty value carrier:
the empty sample table exists, the empty assignment is unique, and extension
preserves it. ppg_product_restriction_examples checks disjoint coordinate
blocks with different value domains, an empty update, event factorization,
and measurability of extension. ppg_probability_examples constructs a point
law on the natural numbers, checks its pushforward to bool, and constructs
coordinate laws on different singleton domains. It also proves that an empty
carrier admits no normalized probability measure.

The remaining order of work is:
  1. Connect subtype coordinate sigma algebras to productSigma, then construct
     the infinite product of the coordinate probability laws. Finite-dimensional
     consistency and countable additivity must be proved before applying the
     Hahn-Kolmogorov extension theorem. Then prove marginals, independence of
     disjoint queries, and the required event bounds.
  2. Port witness-tree conversions, canonicalization, countability, counting,
     and summability of the actual tree weights.
  3. Prove occurrence/expectation bounds, interchange of nonnegative sums and
     expectations, almost-sure termination, and the probabilistic repair link.
