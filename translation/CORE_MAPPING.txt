Directed PPG core translation
Source: ppg-lean d69fd93b9a990d1bbe0ed1630170e44fcbe45982

The first package covers PPGraph.lean (22 results), PPGraphCategorical.lean
(10 results), and PPGraphRR.lean (9 results). Per-result mappings are in
proofs.tsv. The mapping below records the representation and assumptions;
PVS checks the target proofs, not a mechanically verified Lean-to-PVS compiler.

PPGraph.lean

directed_pp_graph.Graph retains the same vertices and directed edges, with
the endpoint-membership condition as a record subtype. No symmetry or
nonemptiness of the vertex type is assumed. The earlier PVS definitions
of pp_edge, pp_edge_weak, pp_valid and pp_valid_weak are unchanged.

Prewalk packages a positive length and a function from below(length) to V.
This represents a Lean pair of n and Prewalk V n: the n = 0 case has no
inhabitant in Lean because len_pos requires n >= 1. All walk vertices,
consecutive edges, invariant conditions and injectivity requirements are
preserved. In particular, a singleton path must satisfy its invariant even
though it has no consecutive edge. Connectivity quantifies over these
packed finite paths; separation and certificate chains use the same paths.

is_subgraph, pp_transform, invariant_set, violation_set, violating,
deterministic and certificate_chain preserve the Lean definitions.
pp_transform uses is_subgraph as a definitional abbreviation for the two
membership-preservation conditions. Its antisymmetry theorem concludes
pointwise equivalence of vertex and edge predicates, as in Lean.

inference_step is a total witness for inference_function_exists. It selects
a valid successor when one exists and otherwise returns its argument.
The choice uses the PVS prelude; no project axiom is introduced. The Lean
proof also uses classical choice. This is not a claim of executable code
extraction or a finite search procedure.

All 22 Lean results keep their names in directed_pp_graph. The additional
is_walk_monotone lemma factors the vertex/edge preservation proof. Three
TCCs establish the endpoint indices and the nonempty set passed to choose.

PPGraphCategorical.lean

categorical_pp_graphs.Graph_T and Graph_U now alias the common directed
Graph type, including endpoint membership. The old unconstrained raw
record types did not record that condition. Existing callers retain the
G1/G2, rr_T/rr_U and inv_T/inv_U interface; these are arbitrary theory
constants corresponding to the explicit Lean graph and predicate arguments.

morphism and embedding expose those arguments explicitly for composition.
pp_morphism? is their fixed-data instance; embedding_agrees proves the
correspondence for pp_embedding?. The definitions of quotient, refinement
and simulation retain the same surjectivity, vertex and round-trip clauses.
Six existing theorem statements remain, and their saved proofs replay.

categorical_pp_closure supplies morphism_compose, id_morphism,
id_embedding and embedding_compose. Its three carrier types are TYPE,
not TYPE+. The two new existence TCCs for the original G1/G2 constants are
proved by the graph with empty vertex and edge predicates, including when
the underlying carrier is empty.

PPGraphRR.lean

ppg_refinement defines rel_extension and RR for arbitrary relations on
possibly empty types. rel_extension_is_equivalence uses both equivalence
hypotheses. g_consistent_with_RR requires only symmetry on T and reflexivity
on U; f_consistent_with_RR requires only reflexivity on T and symmetry on U.
These are the weaker hypotheses of the corresponding Lean results.

ppg_config defines rr_rel_hom and PPGConfig. The is_equiv proof field is
represented by the subtype equivalence[V] of the le_eq field. The field
functions and invariant remain arbitrary. The configuration operations map
as follows:
  PPGConfig.rr_rel           -> config_rr
  PPGConfig.valid            -> config_valid
  PPGConfig.pp_edge_concrete -> pp_edge_concrete
The six remaining Lean results are in ppg_config, with their namespace
prefix omitted from the local PVS formula name. rr_rel_id and
config_id_reduces retain the Lean implication; their names do not assert
an additional converse.

ppg_refinement_nasa proves that rel_extension and RR equal the existing
NASA definitions on the NASA theory's domain (TYPE+ carriers and
equivalence relations). The main translation does not inherit these
stronger requirements. The NASA files are not modified.

Validation and reproduction

Run from the repository root after loading the installed environment:
  source ~/.zprofile
  python3 translation/check_core.py

The script copies the current .pvs/.prf files to a new context, omits PVS
caches, disables oracles and replays the saved .prf scripts. It checks
individual formula statuses, expected declarations, totals and errors;
a successful process exit alone does not count as a proof. It leaves
the working-tree source and proof files untouched and records their hashes.

The first clean replay is stored under:
  ~/.local/state/pvs-checks/directed-core-20261013-final/
All 61 formulas are proved-complete: 50 in the six core/bridge theories,
including 41 mapped Lean results, and 11 in two concrete check theories.
The concrete checks cover asymmetric edges, healthy and unhealthy singleton
walks, graph extension, and an explicitly empty carrier.

Existing certification consumers are checked separately against the saved
pre-change snapshot. Their preexisting incomplete dependencies must not be
counted as closed merely because their scripts finish. Regression results
are recorded under directed-core-20261013-121542/regression-comparison.json
in the same local pvs-checks directory.

The six files are parametric_certification, cert_blocking, cert_quotient,
cert_selection, cert_quotient_bridge and ppg_assessment_bridge. All 110
formula statuses and process results agree before and after the change:
94 proved-complete and 16 proved-incomplete. No new failure was introduced.

There are no new project AXIOM or ASSUMING declarations in this package.
This package does not translate the remaining 188 Lean modules or settle
the unrelated legacy Sterbenz/application proof obligations.
